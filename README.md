Project Vision

CommunityConnect AI is an AI-powered career companion built specifically for Nigerian students, fresh graduates, NYSC members, and early-career professionals.

Unlike Google or traditional job boards, CommunityConnect AI should understand the user's background, career goals, skills, education, and location, then recommend opportunities with clear explanations of why each recommendation matches the user.

The product should feel like a trusted career mentor rather than a search engine.

Core Problem

Students and fresh graduates spend hours searching across many websites and social media platforms for:

 Jobs

 Internships

 SIWES / IT Placements

 Scholarships

 Grants

 Bootcamps

 Graduate trainee programmes

 Learning opportunities

Information is scattered, difficult to trust, and difficult to organise.

CommunityConnect AI solves this by providing personalised AI-powered recommendations in one place.
